How Our Funding System Works

Our initiative is built on two core goals: creating high-quality olympiad experiences for students worldwide, and supporting charitable causes through education.

Revenue Distribution

After operational expenses are covered, the remaining net proceeds are distributed as follows:

75%

Donated to selected charity organizations and educational support initiatives

25%

Used to support the platform team — problem authors, reviewers, technical maintenance, and future development

Operational Expenses

Operational expenses are covered before revenue distribution. These may include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure
  • Domain and platform services
  • Certificate generation
  • Payment processing fees
  • Contest administration
  • Technical development and maintenance

Transparency

We are committed to maintaining full transparency regarding:

  • Total contest revenue
  • Operational expenses
  • Donation amounts
  • Supported organizations

Periodic financial summaries and donation confirmations are published publicly.

Our Mission

We believe olympiad education can be used not only to challenge students academically, but also to create positive real-world impact through charitable support and global student collaboration.
