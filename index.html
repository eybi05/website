What We Are

Where Academia Meets Impact

The International Charity Olympiad bridges world-class academic competition with meaningful charitable giving — proving that the brightest minds can also be the most generous.

🌍

Global Participation

Students from every continent compete on equal footing, earning recognition on the world stage regardless of their school's resources.

⚖️

Fair Competition

Two-round structure with MCQ and FRQ components ensures a rigorous, multidimensional assessment of each student's abilities.

❤️

Charity Impact

Every registration fee and voluntary donation flows directly to verified charity partners. Compete and contribute simultaneously.

📚

Educational Access

A portion of proceeds funds educational materials in underserved communities — turning competition into opportunity for everyone.

Charity System

How Charity Points Work

Academic rank and charity rank stay visibly separate. Donations never buy victory — they build impact.

01

Exam Purchase

Buying the exam automatically grants 5 charity points — because the $5 exam fee goes directly to charity.

+5 pts on purchase
02

Additional Donation

Every extra dollar donated equals 1 charity point. Points cap at 100 — donations beyond that are still accepted and appreciated.

$1 = 1 pt (max 100)
03

Referral Bonus

Share your unique referral link. Each time someone registers using it, you earn +2 charity points automatically.

+2 pts per referral
ℹ️

The Impact Recognition Ranking combines academic score and charity points to celebrate well-rounded participants — but individual academic and charity leaderboards remain fully separate and transparent.
